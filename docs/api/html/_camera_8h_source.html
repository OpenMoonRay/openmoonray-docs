63 /// Returns the transforms to go between world space, render space and
64 /// camera-space at shutter-open (t=0) (where t is expressed in ray time;
65 /// see createRayImpl() below).
66 /// Whether we render from the center/left/right stereo view, camera space
67 /// is always the same and defined by the center camera (as when stereo is
68 /// disabled). The camera view translation due to the interocular distance
69 /// is part of the camera projection transform (C2S).
